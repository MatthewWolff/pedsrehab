Our Mission

Our mission is to improve function, independence, comfort, and participation for all children and adults with disabilities.

We strive to help families navigate the maze of medical, therapy, equipment, recreational, and vocational options for their children.

Welcome

Pediatric Rehabilitation Medicine Associates is an independent medical practice with a main clinic in Milwaukee and satellite clinics in Green Bay and De Pere. We serve patients from childhood through adulthood, manage wheelchair and equipment needs, and collaborate with local therapy providers to deliver coordinated, whole-person care.

We accept Medicaid.
